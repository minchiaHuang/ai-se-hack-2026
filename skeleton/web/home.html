Built For Refugee Employment

Breaking the language barrier, one resume at a time.

Bridgework helps NGO caseworkers turn a refugee client's spoken story — in their own language — into a job-ready English resume. No typing, no formal English required on the client's side.

How it works

  1. 1

    Conversation

    A refugee client speaks in their own language, in a real interview with a caseworker.

  2. 2

    Resume

    AI drafts a resume in Australian English — the caseworker reviews and confirms.

  3. 3

    Match & Apply

    Matched to real, open roles, with a resume ready to send.
