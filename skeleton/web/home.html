How it works

From a conversation to an opportunity.

    Listen together

    Match with evidence

The skills are already there

A kitchen, a warehouse, a family shop — it all counts.

Years of work often disappear on an Australian resume because they were never written down in English. BridgeWork names those skills in the words employers use, and shows where each one came from.
