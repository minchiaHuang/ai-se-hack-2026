The problem
The problem is simple but urgent: people who want to do the right thing with unwanted items
find it too hard to identify sustainable solutions. When reuse, repair, or recycling options are
confusing or inaccessible, landfill becomes the easy answer.

Existing resources, such as council websites, focus narrowly on recycling and are often
outdated. As noted by Dr Alison Gill, Western Sydney University: “we are currently facing a
lack of congruence between the ideal of a circular economy and its logistical reality.”

Hands on services echo this. Reverse Garbage receives daily offers of items they cannot
accept, such as tech, e-waste, or unwearable textiles, but have nowhere reliable to redirect
donors. Similarly, Louise Grace, COO of The Reconnect Project, reports that people are
more likely to dump items when they are not provided with clear alternatives.

The implications go far beyond frustration. Illegal dumping costs NSW residents an
estimated $300 million in lost amenity each year (NSW Illegal Dumping Prevention Strategy
2022–27). Meanwhile, Greater Sydney’s landfill space is projected to run out by 2030 (EPA
NSW). NSW generates one-third of Australia’s waste, forecast to grow from 21 million
tonnes in FY2021 to nearly 37 million tonnes by FY2041.

The NSW EPA highlights a key marker of success as the point where “community members
know how to dispose of, re-use and donate waste and unwanted goods responsibly.”

NSW's landfill crisis has been building for years, but three things converged recently that
make this solvable now. Greater Sydney's landfill space runs out by 2030, creating
regulatory urgency the state can't ignore. NSW's Circular Economy Policy now actively
prioritizes resource recovery, meaning government and corporate buyers are hunting for
solutions that keep stuff out of landfill. Waste is forecast to nearly double by 2041, so the
window to build infrastructure that redirects it is closing fast.

The solution
REcheck is an online directory platform that makes it easy for Australians to find the next
step for their stuff – sustainably.

A quick search will keep items in use for longer and out of landfill by connecting them with
trusted local services for reuse, repair, repurpose, rent and recycling.

REcheck is a purpose-driven start-up creating a simple, accessible way to slow resource
consumption which offers economical, environmental and social benefits.

A simple “REcheck before they chuck” will offer sustainable alternatives for everything from
bread tags to bed sheets, furniture to fashion, phones to pencils, rubber bands to rope, toys
to timber.


Our core product is the platform which features:

   •   A searchable, geolocated database of trusted sustainability services, covering
       everything from clothing donation to e-waste recycling, tool libraries, furniture
       repurposing, and repair hubs.
   •   Clear, standardised information about each service: what items are accepted,
       conditions, costs (or profits), and how to donate or deliver. Further developments of
       the platform include functionality to streamline donations.
   •   Education and campaigns that encourage the behaviour change from consumer to
       custodian by making it easier for people to choose circular solutions over landfill.



Our core markets are:

   •   Givers: Individuals wanting convenient, guilt-free ways to part with items responsibly.
       Eg, families with growing, empty nesters downsizing, schools and businesses
       rebranding, eco-heroes collecting still useful oddments like bread tags or broccoli
       bands, declutterers, renovators, merging households with two of everything.



   •   Takers: Those who accept items for reuse, repair, rent (share or borrow), repurpose
       and recycling. REcheck will provide them with greater visibility and reach, therefore
       more resources.
       Takers include creative reuse centres, libraries of all sorts (tool, toy, street, beach,
       kitchen...), animal shelters, auction houses, paint reuse programs, artists &
       upcyclers, tip shops, community organisations, party kit libraries, education
       providers, repair cafes, recyclers.

How does it work?
For givers
Read https://recheck.au/how-to



For takers

If a taker approaches REcheck, they are encouraged to complete this form -
https://tally.so/r/3jY6da
Alternatively, Leah manually adds all the info on takers gleaned from the taker’s website

Behind the scenes, takers have created their profiles outlining their services, making clear
what they want, and how they will accept it, and of course their contact details.

Along comes the giver. They start by typing in the item they want to say goodbye to, their
location and the distance they’re happy to travel.

Up come their results categorised by all the REs. They can filter them by:

   •   RE
   •   condition of the item
   •   whether they want to post it, drop off local or have it picked up.
   •   And those who offer a way of making money.

AI Hackathon challenges
A. Automate the addition of takers to REcheck
This means collecting the data that is shown in the Admin Platform schema.

Place this info in a ‘holding pattern’ for manual approval so the data can be reviewed before
it is added to the admin system and CRM.

Add a cross-referencing tool that offers a verification score to avoid adding chatbot
generated platforms that appear to be scam tools. Many pop up in my socials feeds. Check
data against public sources (ABN lookup, social presence/followers, news mentions) to flag
inconsistencies and build a confidence/trust score. Give different sources different values,
eg. 97% confident. Lower scores indicate further human investigation required before the
taker is added to REcheck.

While reviewing a taker's profile/story data, suggest blog post angles, social posts or
marketing content ideas.

Add the ability to schedule this collection and input process at specified times, and/or on a
regular basis.

The CRM would ideally contain the contact details for the person responsible for the taker’s
information being keep up to date. They will be contacted regular to invite them to make
changes/ updates.

Once the listing is verified, automate an email to be sent to the taker to let them know they
have been added and invite them to make any changes.

Provided: List of takers that haven’t yet been added to REcheck



B. Make it easy for takers to add themselves
Replace static Tally forms (https://tally.so/r/3jY6da) with an AI chatbot that naturally
interviews takers and extracts structured data from the conversation to complete the taker
listing. The goal is higher completion rates, with it being more engaging for takers.


C. Search for new takers
Collect potential takers that I have not yet identified.

    •   Profile takers that have been added and look for similar
    •   Find people/organisations using upcycled materials – artists, sculptors, jewellers,
        creatives. Eg, people who use board game pieces to make jewellery, crafters who
        use t-shirt yarn, or who harvest yarn from unwearable knitted sweaters.
    •   Identify businesses that purchase items that others throw away. For example, rubber
        bands, bubble wrap, packaging boxes. Wouldn’t it be cool if you knew you could take
        your bubble wrap around the corner to a small business who spends money buying it
        new? For example, picture framers, ceramicists.

## 後台 schema（讀圖轉寫，非原文）

第 8–14 頁是 `admin.recheck.au` 的截圖。以下欄位由我逐格讀圖轉寫，`*` 表示畫面上標示的必填。
這是挑戰 A「自動化新增 taker」要填滿的目標結構。

### Taker（Create a taker）

| 欄位 | 必填 | 備註 |
|---|---|---|
| Name | ✅ | |
| Slug | ✅ | 名稱的 URL 友善版本，僅小寫字母、數字、連字號；用於網址 `/takers/your-taker-slug` |
| Status | ✅ | 預設 Active |
| Short Description | ✅ | 列表頁顯示的一句話 |
| Long Description | ✅ | |
| Logo URL | ✅ | |
| Public Phone / Public Email / Website | | |
| Facebook / Instagram / TikTok | | 列表頁以圖示呈現 |

列表頁欄位：ID、Name、Short Description、Status、Website、Social Media；可用搜尋詞、Categories、Status 篩選。

### Service（Add Service）— 一個 taker 可有多個

| 欄位 | 必填 | 備註 |
|---|---|---|
| RE Type | ✅ | 複選：REuse / REpair / REnt / REpurpose / REcycle |
| Status | ✅ | |
| Category | ✅ | 下拉 |
| Accepted items | ✅ | 自由文字 |
| Paused items | | 自由文字 |
| Search Data | | 由 active 與 paused items 以 `,` 和 `;` 為分隔自動斷成詞或片語，可手動改分組；按鈕為 GENERATE SEARCH DATA |
| Condition of items | ✅ | both／usable／unusable，預設 both |
| Condition of items - description | | |
| Service Fee（勾選）＋ description | | |
| Earn money（勾選）＋ description | | |
| Helpful tips | | |

### Exchange（Add Exchange）— 欄位依類型而異
